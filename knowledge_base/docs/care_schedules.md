# Ideal Care Schedules

## Daily walk timing for dogs
Most adult dogs do well with 2 walks a day -- one in the morning (before
breakfast is fine for most dogs) and one in the early evening. Puppies and
high-energy breeds may need 3 shorter walks instead of 2 long ones. Avoid
strenuous exercise right after a large meal to reduce bloat risk in
deep-chested breeds (e.g. Great Danes, German Shepherds).

## Feeding schedule consistency
Feeding pets at the same times every day helps regulate digestion and makes
housetraining/litter habits more predictable. Puppies and kittens need more
frequent, smaller meals; adults typically do well on 2 meals a day spaced
8-12 hours apart.

## Medication timing
Medications, especially ones tied to meals (e.g. many pain, thyroid, or
allergy medications), should be given at the same time each day for
consistent blood levels. Mark medication tasks as "required" so they're
never skipped due to a full schedule.

## Grooming frequency
Short-haired breeds typically need brushing 1-2x/week; long-haired or
double-coated breeds often need daily brushing, especially during seasonal
shedding. Bathing frequency depends on breed and lifestyle but over-bathing
can dry out skin -- once every 4-6 weeks is a reasonable default absent
other guidance.

## Enrichment and mental stimulation
Cats and high-energy dogs benefit from daily enrichment activities (puzzle
feeders, short training sessions, play) separate from walks/feeding. Even
10-15 minutes a day reduces boredom-related destructive behavior.

## Vet checkups
Healthy adult pets typically need an annual wellness exam; senior pets
(7+ for large dogs, 10+ for small dogs/cats) often benefit from twice-yearly
checkups to catch age-related issues earlier.
